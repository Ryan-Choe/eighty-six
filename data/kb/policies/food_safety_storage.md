# Storage and holding

## Cold holding
Walk-in holds at or below 41F (5C). Anything above that for more than
2 hours gets tossed, no exceptions.

## Fresh mozzarella
Keep in its brine, walk-in only. Once a case is opened, 5 days maximum,
dated on the lid. Never freeze fresh mozzarella; it wrecks the texture.

## Dough
Thawed dough holds 48 hours in the walk-in before quality drops. Room
temperature proofing counts against the 48 hours once it starts.

## Rotation
FIFO on everything. New stock goes behind old stock, every delivery,
every time.
