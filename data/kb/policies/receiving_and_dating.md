# Receiving deliveries

## At the door
Temp-check anything refrigerated before signing: reject above 41F (5C).
Frozen goods showing thaw-and-refreeze (ice crystals, misshapen packs)
get rejected on the spot.

## Date labels
Everything gets a received date on arrival. Opened ready-to-eat items
get a use-by date 7 days out, day of opening counts as day one.

## Shortages
Count cases against the invoice before the driver leaves. Note shortages
on the invoice copy; photograph it. Roma credits automatically, Cascade
needs the photo same-day.
