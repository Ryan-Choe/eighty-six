# Cascade Produce — standing order terms

Local produce house. Rep: Marcus (sales@cascadeproduce.example).

## Delivery schedule
Delivers daily Monday through Saturday. Orders in by 8pm arrive next
morning before 9am. No Sunday delivery.

## Minimums and fees
No minimum order. Orders under $100 carry a $15 delivery fee.

## Quality credits
Bad produce gets credited if you send a photo within 24 hours of
delivery. After 24 hours the case is closed.
