# Roma Foods — account terms

Broadline distributor. Account #4471, rep: Gina (gina@romafoods.example).

## Delivery schedule
Delivery days are Tuesday, Thursday, and Saturday, morning window 7-11am.
Orders placed by 11pm the night before a delivery day make the next
morning's truck. Miss the cutoff and you wait for the next delivery day.

## Minimums and fees
Minimum order is $250. Orders under the minimum can still be placed but
carry a $25 small-order fee. No delivery fee above the minimum.

## Shorted items and credits
If the truck shorts an item, credit is automatic on the next invoice. No
need to call. Substitutions are never sent without a call first.

## Payment
Net-15 on invoice. Card on file accepted with no surcharge.
